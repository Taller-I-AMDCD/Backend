# Planner–Executor evaluation protocol

## Scope

This protocol measures the existing Planner–Executor implementation without changing its rules, tools, datasets, or stopping behavior. It produces quantitative evidence and does not assign a comparative score or architectural recommendation.

## Scenarios

The evaluation uses `consistent_relation.csv`, `confounded_relation.csv`, and `outlier_relation.csv`. Every dataset contains 500 records and is evaluated with `X` and `Y` as targets. No control variable is supplied manually; the Planner receives metadata generated by the normal profiling action.

All scenarios use the same Planner, Executor, Orchestrator, registered tools, and `max_iterations = 8` configuration.

## Repetitions and timing

Each scenario runs three warm-ups followed by twenty measured repetitions. Warm-ups are excluded from timing metrics. Measurements use `time.perf_counter()` around in-memory orchestration; CSV loading is outside the timed section.

The evaluation reports minimum, maximum, mean, median, and nearest-rank p95 execution time. Every repetition must produce an identical functional trace for its scenario; only execution time may vary.

## Behavioral checks

- Consistent relation: Pearson, Spearman, and filtered correlation remain strong.
- Confounded relation: Pearson is strong and partial correlation is at most 30% of its magnitude.
- Outlier relation: observations are removed and filtered correlation is at most 50% of the original magnitude.
- Every successful scenario must end with `NO_NEW_ACTIONS`, contain no failed execution, and provide a complete trace.

Thresholds evaluate qualitative behavior rather than exact reference values.

## Trace completeness

Every iteration must retain the Planner action identifier, tool, parameters, reason, Executor status, tool, parameters, result, and error. The final record must include a stop reason.

## Structural metrics

Productive modules are top-level Python files in `src/`, excluding `__init__.py` and demonstration modules. Approximate productive LOC counts non-empty, non-comment-only lines. Test count represents discovered `test_*` function definitions. These measurements are descriptive and do not imply quality rankings.
